KDP Ads Manager Privacy Notice

Effective date: 2 August 2026

KDP Ads Manager is a private, internal application used by its operator to manage and analyse advertising for the operator's own Kindle Direct Publishing catalogue. It is not offered to the public or to other advertisers.

Information the application accesses

After the operator authorises access through Amazon, the application may access Amazon Ads account and profile identifiers, campaign settings, budgets, bids, keywords, targets, placements, search terms, and advertising performance reports for the authorised advertising account.

How information is used

The information is used only to authenticate the operator, produce internal reports and dashboards, identify opportunities to improve advertising performance, and—when separately enabled by the operator—manage campaigns, bids, targets, keywords, and budgets.

Sharing and sale of information

The application does not sell personal information or advertising data. It does not share Amazon Ads data with unauthorised third parties. Data may be processed only by infrastructure providers needed to operate the application, subject to their security and privacy protections.

Storage and security

Application credentials and advertising data are kept in systems controlled by the operator. Reasonable technical safeguards are used to protect credentials and data, and access is limited to the operator.

Retention and deletion

Advertising data is kept only while needed for internal reporting and campaign management. The operator can revoke the application's Amazon authorisation and delete locally stored data at any time.

Changes to this notice

This notice may be updated if the application's data practices change. The effective date above will be revised when material changes are made.

Contact

Privacy questions can be sent to the operator through the contact method associated with the account or website hosting this notice.
